A GTD workflow

From “remember this” to “done”.

Capture first. Decide what it means when you have time to think.

01 / Capture

Get it out of your head.

Add “sort out the garden” to your inbox. You don't have to choose a project or a date yet.

02 / Organize

Make the next step clear.

Turn the idea into a project: “Plant the balcony garden”. Start with “Measure the planter space”, then order the steps that follow.

03 / Plan

Fit the work to your day.

Choose a few actions for today and add time estimates. Leave room for the rest of your day.

04 / Review

Give quiet projects some attention.

Check the projects due for review. Look at what has moved, what is waiting, and whether each project has a useful next action.

A few useful answers.

Do I need an account?

No. Choose Try locally to start in your browser. Create an account if you want a hosted workspace.

What works without a connection?

You can work with tasks stored on your device. Sync, AI and other online integrations need a connection. Images and attachments need to be available locally before you go offline.

Where is my data?

Local use stores data in your browser at the address you use. A connected workspace also sends data to its sync server. See the privacy policy and data guide for details.

Can I run my own server?

Yes. The Docker guide covers setup. You are responsible for operating and backing up your server.

How do I return to my hosted workspace?

Open the workspace address you received when you signed up. You sign in at that address. Bookmark it to return.
